The problem isn't laziness — it's that existing tools are passive containers that ignore your cognitive state.

## What is the Execution Gap?

It's that frustrating distance between your plans and your results. You know what you *should* be doing, but you find yourself scrolling through social media or cleaning your desk instead.

### Friction is the Enemy

Most productivity tools require too much setup:
- Choosing a workspace
- Selecting a template
- Adding tags
- Setting priority levels

By the time you've organized the task, you've already lost the spark of motivation.

## How FluxOS Bridges the Gap

FluxOS is designed to be an **active** partner. Instead of just holding your tasks, it helps you execute them by:

1. **Frictionless Capture**: Record your intent in under 2 seconds.
2. **AI Decomposition**: Automatically break down vague goals into concrete steps.
3. **Energy Matching**: Showing you only what you can handle *right now*.

Stop planning. Start doing.
